# Merchant acceptance task

This task uses fictional records. Nothing is submitted to PayPal.

Open the review page at the local URL supplied by the coordinator in a fresh browser profile or private window. Do not read the repository walkthrough, tests, fixture files or evaluation key before completing the task.

## Your task

Act as the merchant preparing a response to the account limitation shown in the case. Use the application to:

1. Decide what the supplied records establish about the payment and the work it covered.
2. Identify anything that still needs clarification or more evidence. Note the records that led you to that conclusion.
3. Review the proposed claims and make the decisions you consider appropriate.
4. Download the reviewed packet, open both files, and check whether they accurately represent your decisions and the remaining questions.

Think aloud if convenient. Work independently; the coordinator may resolve an access problem but should not explain the case or direct you to a finding. If you get stuck, describe where and why.

## Report your experience

Give the coordinator these notes and the downloaded ZIP:

- Your name or reviewer identifier, test time, browser, and any prior familiarity with this project or case.
- What you concluded, what remains unresolved, and the citations you used.
- Which claims you accepted or rejected and why.
- Whether the downloaded PDF and JSON agree with your decisions and the evidence.
- Any confusing step, failure, or help you needed.

Use only the fictional demo records. Do not include credentials or real merchant documents in your report.
